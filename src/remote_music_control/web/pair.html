Pair a phone or computer

On the device you want to use as a remote, connect to the same Wi-Fi as this PC, then point its camera at a code below and open the link. The music controls open and the device remembers the access.

Any phone

Uses this PC's address, $ip_address. Works on Android and iPhone.

iPhone or computer

$name_qr

Uses this PC's name, $name_address. Keeps working if the address changes. Android browsers can't open it.

These codes contain the access token: anyone who scans them can control the music. Don't share photos of this screen.

Stop this PC's address from changing (optional)

Your router gives this PC its address, and it may give a different one some day — for example after a power cut. A phone paired with the old address then stops connecting. A DHCP reservation tells the router to always give this PC the same address. You set it once; nothing else changes for anyone.

  1. On this PC, open the router's settings page — usually $router_address. The admin password is often printed on a sticker on the router.
  2. Find the DHCP settings. Routers name them differently: DHCP, LAN, Reserva de endereço, Reserva DHCP, IP fixo, Address Reservation or Static lease. Many routers also list connected devices with a button to reserve their address.
  3. Add a reservation with the MAC address and the address above, and save.

If your router doesn't offer this, or you don't have its password, skip it: whenever the address changes, come back to this page and scan again.
